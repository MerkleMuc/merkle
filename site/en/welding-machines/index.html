Welding machines

Five processes, one supplier.

From thin sheet metal to structural steel: we stock machines for every process — and you may try each of them before you buy.

  1. Home
  2. Welding machines

The processes

Which process suits your workpiece.

The detailed German pages list every series. The machine finder is available in German as well.

MIG/MAG

MAG welding is mainly used on steel and stainless steel, MIG welding on non-ferrous metals. Our shielding gas machines handle both — from thin sheet to large components. Five series, from the economical RedMIG to the SpeedMIG.

TIG

Shielding gas welding with markedly less heat and an inert gas. That makes TIG suitable for every metal that can be fusion welded. From portable DC machines of 5.0 kg up to the Insquare industrial line.

Stick (MMA)

Manual metal arc welding is one of the oldest processes and is used mainly on site today. Small, light and powerful: MobiARC and LiteArc 180 with LiftTIG ignition and a digital display, only 5.0 kg.

Pulse-Arc

The pulsed arc allows comparatively thick wire electrodes. The range runs from a portable power source to automated special solutions, for shipbuilding or a production line.

Plasma cutting

With compressed air as the plasma gas you cut almost any conductive metal — mild steel, chrome-nickel steel, aluminium, brass, copper, cast iron or titanium. An economical alternative to oxy-fuel cutting, with a narrow kerf and little distortion.

Try before you buy

Test any machine in our showroom or on your own workpiece in your workshop. Machines can also be rented first and bought afterwards.

The full catalogue of series, technical details and accessories is on the German pages: welding machines, filler metals, accessories and gases. Our machine finder is in German too — or simply call us, we speak English.
