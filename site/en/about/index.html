Who we are

We do not simply deliver products.

Merkle Schweißtechnik began in 1976, when Bartholomäus Burghart started covering the Munich area. The limited company followed in 1980. Today the company is family-run with its succession settled, and it supplies welding and cutting technology from three locations in Bavaria.

We see ourselves as problem solvers in all questions around welding and cutting — whether it is a tricky welding job, a rental machine needed within hours or an express delivery to Bolzano.

Company history

Five years in which everything
was decided.

1976

The beginning of Merkle Munich: Bartholomäus Burghart starts travelling the Munich area.

1980

The first milestone: our first shop in Peslmüllerstraße.

1994

Space became tight: we move into a larger building in Bodenseestraße.

2008

Now we build ourselves: we move into our own building in Anton-Böck-Straße.

2013

Siegfried Awissus is named entrepreneur of the year at the CSU ball in Bavaria.
