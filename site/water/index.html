Coolant ME 30-100.2-ECO

pH 7.8, conductivity below 25 microsiemens, frost protection down to −30 °C.

  1. Startseite
  2. Coolant

Why it matters

The wrong coolant costs you the pump —
and the torch along with it.

Why is it inherently important to use only the ME 30-100.2-ECO coolant?

Since a welding or cutting system is not of little value and every user is interested in it doing its work for a long time without great expense, the use of the right coolant is of extreme importance. The ME 30-100.2-ECO coolant ensures that the high level of corrosion protection it contains optimally protects all important components, especially the susceptible non-ferrous heavy metals used in welding and cutting systems.

Why is a proper coolant especially important in the TIG area?

For the operational lifetime of a TIG torch it is very important which coolant is used. Perfect ignition requires a very low conductivity combined with an optimally balanced pH value of 7.8. This is the case with the Merkle coolant. Coolant with a pH value of less than 6 is considered acidic and aggressive and primarily destroys components in the pump and in the torch.

What does the water pump have to do with the coolant?

The operational lifetime depends to a large extent on which coolant is used. Using the wrong coolant will cause damage to seal and shaft in a very short time. A leaking water pump causes system failures with expensive downtime and high repair costs.

Properties

Six figures that make the difference.

pH value 7.8

Optimally balanced for perfect ignition in the TIG area. Values below 6 are considered acidic and aggressive.

Conductivity below 25 microsiemens

Attacks neither the current-water cable nor the water pump. The recommended maximum is 100 to 150 microsiemens.

Frost protection to −30 °C

Usable in unheated vehicles and containers.

No flash or ignition point

Declaring one is not legally required — a considerable advantage for storage and transport.

No dangerous goods labelling

Meets all transport regulations worldwide without labelling, for example ADR, ADN and IMDG.

Biodegradable

Biodegradable according to OECD criteria. No health hazard under EC Regulation 1272/2008 CLP.
